Privacy Policy

Effective October 7, 2026

PickleDeals is operated by JD Innovations LLC, a Florida company (“we”, “us”). Questions about this policy or your data: support@pickledeals.app.

The short version

  • We collect what the app needs to work: your email, a display name, what you post and save, and the messages you send in the app.
  • We never store your exact location. “Near you” uses an area of about 1 km. The only exact point we store is a meet-up spot you choose to send in a chat, and only the person you’re chatting with can see it.
  • We don’t sell your data, show third-party ads or track you across other companies’ apps and websites.
  • Some store links are affiliate links: if you buy, the store may pay us a commission. We never pass your account details to stores.
  • You can delete your account at any time in the app.

Photos. Before upload, the app re-saves your photos as new images, which removes camera metadata, including GPS location.

Location. When you allow location access, the app sends your device’s position with a search. Our server rounds it to a grid cell of about 1 km, uses it for that search and doesn’t store it. If you save a home area, we store only that grid cell. Listings are shown at the centre of their ~1 km cell, never at an address. A meet-up spot you send in a chat is an exact point: it starts at your current position unless you move the map, and only the person you send it to can see it.

What we don’t do

  • No advertising SDKs, data brokers or cross-app tracking. We don’t sell or rent personal data, and we don’t “share” it for targeted advertising.
  • The app doesn’t use analytics or crash-reporting services today. If we add them, we’ll update this policy first and they will never receive your location.
  • This website doesn’t use cookies or analytics.

Who we share data with

  • Other users: your display name, when you joined, your listings (with their ~1 km area) and the messages and offers you send them.
  • Service providers that run PickleDeals for us and process data only on our behalf: Supabase (database, storage and sign-in), Resend (sign-in emails), Expo and Apple (push notifications), Apple (Sign in with Apple) and Vercel (this website).
  • Stores and affiliate networks: when you tap Get deal, you go to the store through a link that identifies PickleDeals, not you. After that, the store’s own privacy policy applies. One of the stores in the app, Pickleball Grip Doctor, is owned by the same people as PickleDeals; it receives your information only if you shop there.
  • Legal: when the law requires it, or to protect people’s safety.

How long we keep data

  • Account data: until you delete your account.
  • Deleting your account permanently removes your account, profile, listings and their photos, saves, alerts, notification settings and push tokens. Conversations about your listings, and their photos, are deleted. Messages you sent about other people’s listings stay in their inbox, without your name. Store clicks are kept without any link to you. If you used Sign in with Apple, we revoke the app’s access with Apple.
  • Reports may be kept for up to 2 years after they’re resolved, where we need them for safety or legal reasons.
  • Prices from Amazon’s API are deleted within 24 hours, as Amazon’s terms require.

Your choices and rights

  • Edit your profile, home area and notification settings in the app. Turn off location access in iOS Settings at any time.
  • Delete your account in Profile → Account → Delete account.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your data, or to correct or delete it, by emailing support@pickledeals.app. Depending on where you live (for example California), you may have additional rights; we’ll honour them as the law requires and won’t treat you differently for using them.

Age

PickleDeals is for people 18 and older. We don’t knowingly collect information from anyone under 18; if we learn we have, we’ll delete it.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, access is limited by database rules, and staff tools require an admin account. No service is perfectly secure, so please keep your email account and devices secure too.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
