Support

We usually reply within one business day.

Email support@pickledeals.app with your question. If it’s about a listing, a chat or another user, include their display name and what happened.

Report a listing, message or user

In the app, open the listing, chat or profile and tap Report. You can also Block someone so you stop seeing each other. We review reports within 24 hours.

A deal or price looks wrong

Prices and codes come from the stores and can change. The price at the store’s checkout is the one that applies. Prices in the app don’t include tax or shipping, which the store settles at checkout. Tell us which product it was and we’ll check it.

Delete your account

In the app, go to Profile → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and data as described in our Privacy Policy. If you can’t sign in, email us from the address on your account and we’ll delete it for you.

Sign-in codes

Codes come from no-reply@pickledeals.app. If one doesn’t arrive within a couple of minutes, check your spam folder, then request a new one. Never share a sign-in code with anyone, including people who say they’re from PickleDeals.
